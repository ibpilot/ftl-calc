Duty Parameters
When are you acclimatised?
Acclimatised
Your body clock is within <2h of local time at your reporting point.
Typical: operating from your home base, or after sufficient time at destination.
Unknown
You have crossed >2 time zones and have been away from home base for <48h without qualifying rest. Your internal clock is out of sync with local time.
EASA reduces max FDP in this state. Use this option conservatively.
Re-acclimatised
After 48h+ at a new time zone with adequate rest, you are considered re-acclimatised to local time — use Acclimatised again.
BCN → East Coast USA (e.g. JFK, BOS):
Approx. −6h difference. Unknown state for the first 48h after arrival. Layover >48h with good rest → re-acclimatised to EDT.

MAD → East Coast USA (e.g. JFK, MIA):
Same offset, approx. −6h (CET/CEST vs EDT/EST). Identical rule applies — Unknown state on arrival, re-acclimatised after 48h adequate rest at destination.

Spain → West Coast or South America:
Differences of 7–9h. Unknown state applies on arrival. Return leg to Spain: after landing home, recover acclimatisation within 48h of adequate rest.

What is augmented crew?
Standard
The minimum required crew for the aircraft type. On the A330: 2 pilots (Captain + First Officer). No one can leave the flight deck during cruise.
Augmented
3 or more pilots on board, allowing each crew member to take an in-flight rest break while the others operate. The resting pilot is replaced at their seat by a qualified relief pilot.
Enables FDP extensions of up to +3h (without IFR) or more with rest facility class.
A330 transatlantic (e.g. MAD/BCN → JFK, LAX, GRU):
These routes are typically operated with 3 pilots (augmented). Each pilot takes one rest break in the crew bunk or business seat. This allows the FDP to extend beyond the standard 13h limit — enabling ~10h block flights plus ground time within legal limits.

Commander's Discretion (ORO.FTL.205(f))
Discretion
When unforeseen circumstances arise after departure, the commander may extend the FDP beyond the calculated limit. The extension must be reported to the operator.
Standard crew
Maximum extension: +2 hours over the applicable FDP limit.
Augmented crew
Maximum extension: +3 hours over the applicable FDP limit.
Important: Discretion applies to unforeseen circumstances only — ATC delays, weather diversions, technical issues. It cannot be pre-planned in the roster. If a crew member considers themselves unfit due to fatigue, they must not exercise discretion.

Duty encroaches WOCL (02:00–05:59).
Window of Circadian Low (WOCL)
WOCL
The Window of Circadian Low is the period between 02:00 and 05:59 local time when the human body is at its lowest point of alertness and physiological performance. Defined in EASA ORO.FTL.105.
Why it matters
Cognitive performance, reaction time, and decision-making are significantly impaired during the WOCL — even without sleep deprivation. The risk of fatigue-related error is at its peak.
EASA requires operators to reduce maximum FDP when duties encroach this window.
Practical impact:
A report time of 04:00 falls inside the WOCL. Your operator's OM-A will apply a reduced FDP table for WOCL-encroaching duties — typically 1–2h shorter than the standard table shown here. Always cross-check with your Operations Manual when your duty starts or runs through the early morning hours.
